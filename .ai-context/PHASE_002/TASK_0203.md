## 2. Problem

Combat damage is currently dealt in a single step. First strike (Rule 702.7) requires splitting combat damage into two steps when any creature has first strike or double strike. Creatures with first strike deal damage only in the first step, allowing them to kill blockers/attackers before regular damage is dealt. This fundamentally changes combat math.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] When any creature in combat has first strike, combat damage splits into two steps (Rule 510.4)
- [ ] First-strikers assign and deal damage only in the first combat damage step
- [ ] Non-first-strike creatures assign and deal damage only in the second step
- [ ] SBAs are checked between the two steps (creatures with lethal damage are destroyed)
- [ ] A creature killed in the first step does not deal damage in the second step
- [ ] When no creature has first/double strike, combat damage remains a single step (no regression)
- [ ] Unit test: 2/2 first strike vs 4/2 vanilla — first striker survives, vanilla dies before dealing damage

## 4. Implementation Guidance

- Modify the combat damage phase to detect whether first/double strike exists among combatants
- If yes: run first combat damage step (only first-strikers), check SBAs, then run second step (non-first-strikers only)
- If no: single step as today (no behavior change)
- Keep double strike handling for TASK_0204 — here, just ensure the two-step framework exists
- The SBA check between steps must handle lethal damage destruction and trigger queuing
