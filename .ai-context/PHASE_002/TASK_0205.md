## 2. Problem

Currently, all combat damage from a blocked attacker is absorbed by blockers — none reaches the defending player. Trample (Rule 702.19) allows excess damage over lethal to be assigned to the defending player. This introduces a new AI decision point: damage division among blockers and the player.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A blocked attacker with trample can assign excess damage to the defending player
- [ ] Attacker must assign at least lethal damage to each blocker before trampling
- [ ] Lethal = blocker toughness minus damage already marked (from first strike step, if applicable)
- [ ] `IStrategy` exposes a damage assignment decision when trample is relevant
- [ ] Without trample, blocked attacker's damage stays on blockers only (no regression)
- [ ] Trample with no remaining blockers (all removed): all damage to player (Rule 702.19d)
- [ ] Unit test: 6/6 trample blocked by 2/2 — assign 2 to blocker, 4 to player
- [ ] Unit test: 6/6 trample blocked by 3/3 + 2/2 — assign at least 3+2, trample 1

## 4. Implementation Guidance

- Add `IStrategy.DecideDamageAssignment()` or similar — input: attacker, blockers with toughness/existing damage; output: damage per blocker + player damage
- Validate assignment: each blocker must receive >= lethal, sum = attacker power
- For non-trample creatures, auto-assign (no strategy call needed, or constrain player_damage = 0)
- `RandomStrategy` can assign minimum lethal to each blocker in order, remainder to player
