## 2. Problem

There is no life gain mechanism tied to damage dealing. Lifelink (Rule 702.15) causes a creature's controller to gain life equal to the damage it deals. This affects race calculations — a lifelink creature simultaneously deals damage and extends the controller's clock.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] When a creature with lifelink deals combat damage, its controller gains that much life
- [ ] Lifelink applies to damage dealt to both creatures and players
- [ ] Multiple lifelink creatures each trigger life gain independently
- [ ] Lifelink stacks with other keywords: deathtouch + lifelink deals 1 damage but gains life for the full amount dealt
- [ ] Life gain happens simultaneously with damage (same event, not a trigger)
- [ ] Unit test: 3/3 lifelink attacks unblocked — player takes 3, controller gains 3
- [ ] Unit test: 3/3 lifelink blocked by 2/2 — controller gains 3 (damage dealt = power, not blocker toughness)

## 4. Implementation Guidance

- In the damage-dealing step, after applying damage to the recipient, check if source has lifelink
- If yes: add source's controller's life by the amount of damage dealt
- Lifelink is NOT a triggered ability (Rule 702.15b) — it happens as part of damage being dealt, not after
- This means it's a modification of the damage event, not a separate game action
- Attach the life-gain at the event-application chokepoint introduced by `TASK_0403` (Phase 4) — the centralized damage-event point — rather than re-scattering damage logic across combat and spell-damage sites. Lifelink is not a Rule 614 replacement, so it does not register on the chokepoint's replacement list; it is inline post-damage logic hanging off the same centralized event.
