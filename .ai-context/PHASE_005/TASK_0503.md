## 2. Problem

Creatures currently cannot attack or use tap abilities on the turn they enter the battlefield (summoning sickness, Rule 302.6). Haste (Rule 702.10) removes this restriction, allowing immediate attacks and tap ability activation. This affects AI timing decisions for when to deploy threats.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A creature with haste can attack the turn it enters the battlefield
- [ ] A creature with haste can use tap abilities the turn it enters (future-proofing)
- [ ] A creature without haste still cannot attack the turn it enters (no regression)
- [ ] Summoning sickness check respects haste keyword
- [ ] Unit test: creature with haste attacks on entry turn
- [ ] Unit test: creature without haste cannot attack on entry turn (existing behavior preserved)

## 4. Implementation Guidance

- The existing summoning sickness check should already exist (creatures track which turn they entered)
- Add a bypass: if creature `HasKeyword(KeywordAbility.Haste)`, skip summoning sickness
- This should be a simple conditional in the attack legality check
- Summoning sickness affects: attacking and activating abilities with tap/untap symbols in cost

## 5. Files to Modify

- `Source/Kvasir.Engine.UnitTest/` (haste tests)
