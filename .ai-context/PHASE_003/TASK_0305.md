## 2. Problem

`TASK_0304` handles the single-target case, where an illegal target means the whole spell fizzles. Spells with two or more targets behave differently: at resolution, a spell resolves if *at least one* target is still legal, affecting only the legal targets (Rule 608.2c). This partial-fizzle rule is distinct logic and is the reason multi-target is split out. This task also covers the "choose N different targets" constraint at cast time.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A spell/ability requiring multiple targets has all targets chosen at cast time (Rule 601.2c)
- [ ] Multiple targets of the same spell must be different objects unless the spell says otherwise (Rule 601.2c)
- [ ] At resolution, if some (not all) targets are illegal, the spell resolves affecting only the still-legal targets (Rule 608.2c)
- [ ] At resolution, if ALL targets are illegal, the spell fizzles entirely (Rule 608.2b)
- [ ] "Divide" targeting (e.g., damage split among targets) chooses the division at cast time and honors it at resolution (Rule 601.2d) — if in scope for the chosen test card
- [ ] The action enumeration produces distinct legal target *combinations* (documented as a combinatorial-growth source)
- [ ] Unit test: two-target spell, one target removed in response → resolves on the remaining legal target only
- [ ] Unit test: two-target spell, both targets removed → full fizzle

## 4. Implementation Guidance

- Generalize the single-target field on `StackObject` to an ordered target list. Keep single-target as the one-element case so `TASK_0304` behavior is unchanged.
- At resolution, partition targets into legal/illegal, apply effects only to the legal set, and full-fizzle only when the legal set is empty.
- Enforce the "different objects" constraint during target selection.
- Combination enumeration for `GetLegalActions()` is the multi-target action-space explosion; enumerate correctly, defer pruning/mitigation to the Phase 6 AI layer.
- Add a canonical multi-target card as YAML (e.g., a "deal 2 damage divided among one or two targets" spell, or a two-target bounce).
- Key rules: RX-601.2c, RX-601.2d, RX-608.2b, RX-608.2c.
